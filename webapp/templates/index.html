ToM Lens

Where do language models represent who knows what? Belief alignment, cognitive maps and the logit lens, layer by layer.

Triplet alignment by depth

Share of triplets where a prompt sits closer to a different story with the same belief status than to its own one-sentence-different twin. Above 0.5, belief state beats wording. Bands are 95% bootstrap intervals over story groups.

Learned triplet metric vs control

A 16-d linear map trained with triplet loss on training stories, scored on held-out ones. Dashed: the same probe on a control task (random labels per object and slot variant). The gap is selectivity.

Logit lens: when the answer appears

Mean logit(answer) − logit(foil) after projecting each layer's state through the final norm and unembedding. Positive means the right container is winning.

Behaviour: forced-choice accuracy

Correct when the model gives the true answer a higher log-probability than the other container. FB rows are the false-belief cases; chance is 50%.

Cognitive map (classical MDS)

Cosine distances between hidden states at the last prompt token, projected to 2-D. Prompts view: held-out stories, questions about the leaver's belief (circles) and the actor's belief about the leaver (triangles). Depth is relative, so models with different layer counts line up.
